Our 4-Gate Curation Protocol

In an industry saturated with paid influencer threads and opaque tokenomics, credibility requires rigorous filtering. Every educational resource in BlockPulse passes four mandatory checkpoints:

01. Conflict-of-Interest Audit
We verify authors and software vendors have zero undisclosed token allocations or paid promotion agreements.
02. Empirical & On-Chain Falsifiability
Claims must be grounded in verifiable ledger data, orderbook telemetry, or reproducible macroeconomic statistics.
03. Pedagogical Clarity
Complex quantitative reports are distilled into clear, structured takeaways linked directly to course modules.
04. Security & Open-Source Review
Recommended software tools and wallets are evaluated for audit history, non-custodial design, and privacy hygiene.
